    How the scoring works

    Three lenses, each from 0 to 100, built only from your answers. We publish the weights so you can see them, question them and disagree.

    Hypothesis. These weights are our current judgement, not a validated model. We will change them as we learn, and say so here.

    Scoring lenses and weights
    LensWhat goes inWeights
    ValueHow many people, how often, how bad, and the gap left by what exists today30% reach, 25% frequency, 30% severity, 15% gap
    EvidenceThe strongest evidence you have, and how close you are to the problem65% evidence, 35% closeness
    ViabilityHow clear the payer is, signs that someone would pay, and how hard it is to deliver40% payer, 40% signal, 20% delivery
    PriorityThe three lenses together50% value, 25% evidence, 25% viability

    From scores to a next step

    1. Value under 40: narrow it to the people for whom it is worst.
    2. Evidence under 50: gather evidence before building anything.
    3. Viability under 45: run a small test, and make finding the payer part of it.
    4. All three at 60 or more: ready for a pilot with a paying partner.
    5. Otherwise: ready for a small test with the people affected.

    Low evidence is a reason to learn, not a reason to stop. Value counts most because a problem has to be worth solving before anything else matters. The steps follow the five stages Sevarth uses to decide whether an idea earns its next step.
